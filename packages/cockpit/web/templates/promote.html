Connect Telegram bot

Telegram approval bot is required for live trading.

Live trades pause until you approve them from Telegram. Add the following lines to your .env file:

TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=your_bot_token_here TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID=your_chat_id_here

Get a bot token from @BotFather on Telegram. Find your chat id by messaging @userinfobot.

Arm live trading?

Real capital from your Robinhood account will be eligible for orders the next time the worker loop runs. Each trade still requires Telegram approval, and your $300 first-float guardrail remains in effect.

Disarm live trading?

Live orders pause immediately. Open positions are not closed. Provide a reason so future-you knows why this happened.
